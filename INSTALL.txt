SIMPLE WEAPON SWAP 1.2.0
World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 (addon interface 16001)

INSTALL
1. Transfer and extract the ZIP on your WoW device.
2. Copy the simple_weapon_swap folder into your Forever client's Interface/AddOns.
   The path inside the game folder should be:
   _forever_/Interface/AddOns/simple_weapon_swap/simple_weapon_swap.toc
3. Launch WoW Forever and enable Simple Weapon Swap in the AddOns list.

UPGRADE FROM FERAL WEAPON SWAP
To keep your settings, close WoW and open your character's folder at
_forever_/WTF/Account/<account>/<realm>/<character>/SavedVariables. If
simple_weapon_swap.lua does not exist there yet, copy FeralWeaponSwap.lua
to simple_weapon_swap.lua in the same folder. Disable or remove the old
Feral Weapon Swap addon before using Simple Weapon Swap.

SET UP
1. Type /fws in chat out of combat.
2. For each set, pick a two-hander or a one-hander with an eligible
   off-hand item, then click Save selections. You can also equip the weapons
   and click Save equipped.
3. Edit either Set name field and press Enter or click away to save the name.
   Leave the field blank to restore its default name, Damage or Healing.
4. To assign a keyboard shortcut, click Bind key and press an available key or
   combination.

USE AN EXISTING ACTION-BAR KEY
1. Restore or keep the key on its usual slot in WoW's Key Bindings.
2. On the character that should swap weapons, open /fws out of combat
   and enable Override an action-bar key on this character.
3. Click Bind key and press the same key. WoW keeps its original binding.

For example, restore T to Action Bar 2, Slot 1 in WoW's shared Key Bindings.
On your druid, open /fws, enable the override, then click Bind key and press T.
On your mage, put Polymorph in that slot and leave the addon swap key
unassigned. T swaps on the druid and casts Polymorph on the mage.

Enable Pass through to the normal WoW binding out of combat to release
the override and use the regular binding. The addon remembers the swap key.
Disable pass-through to resume keyboard swapping. The on-screen button
works in either mode.

CONTROLS
- Left-click the display or press your active swap key to swap sets.
- Right-click for the second set (Healing by default) or middle-click for the
  first set (Damage by default).
- Hold Shift and drag with the left mouse button to move the display out of
  combat. Your settings and display position are saved per character.
- Hover to see equipped weapons and control hints. Type /fws to reopen setup.

COMBAT SWAPS
Keep an ordinary bag slot free to stow an off-hand. Wait for WoW's weapon-swap
global cooldown before swapping again. If a same-type swap gets rejected or
you changed weapons manually during combat, use right-click or middle-click
to select the intended set directly.

Copies of the same item with different enchants or random stats share a single
choice. If a swap fails, type /fws status for your build and the saved and
equipped item IDs.
